Frame settings

Edge margin

If your frame's mount covers the edge of the screen, draw the picture a few pixels smaller so nothing is hidden. The picture shrinks evenly and a white border of this many pixels is left on every side.

Button (KEY3)

Press the button on the frame to switch to this layout. Press it again, or wait for the next scheduled update, to go back. The new picture takes a few seconds to appear.

Home town (for the weather widgets)

The Weather widgets can show a surf spot or your home. To find the numbers: open Google Maps, right-click your town, and click the two numbers at the top of the menu to copy them (the first is latitude, the second longitude).

When does it change? At the frame's next picture (every 3 hours), or press Render now and wait 3 minutes.

Friends' frames

Each friend's frame has its own layouts, and they choose what it shows. Send them their server address (for the frame's Wi-Fi setup) and the studio link. Their email must also be on the Cloudflare Access list, or they can't log in.

Add a frame

Frame ID: 2–24 lower-case letters, numbers or dashes. It can't be changed later. Separate several emails with commas.
